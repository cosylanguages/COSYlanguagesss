Tactile Learning Ecosystem
Paper, audio & conversation working together seamlessly.
A single Speaking Club session can become a podcast episode, a zine article, and a pocket Leitner card for your commute.

Designed around 3 core pillars (Bridge Tools, COSYzine, and COSY Radio) across 4 printable formats. Everything is 100% offline-ready for A2–B1 language learners who want tangible progress off-screen.

Why this exists

I couldn't help but wonder... why do we call it "screen time" and never "paper time," "walk time," or "porch time"? Somewhere between one lesson and the next, a learner's life doesn't pause — it keeps happening, on trains and kitchen tables and evening walks. Beyond the Screen exists so that a phrase learned on Tuesday can still be practised on Wednesday, even if the laptop stays closed. Not instead of the screen. Just... beyond it, too.

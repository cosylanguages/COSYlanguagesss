🌍 Top 100 A0–A1 Semantic Master List (100 Progressive Knowledge Chains)

C Written by COSY Editorial Team · 📅 Updated September 2026 · ⏱️ 12 min read · 💬 14 Languages Matrix

Organized by real-world human experience rather than random alphabetical lists, this master guide provides 100 progressive semantic chains across 10 core life domains for absolute A0–A1 beginners. Each domain builds directly on the last to expand student communicative capability, ending in a concrete Can-Do Milestone Goal linked live to COSYdata across all 14 target languages.

The starting point for every beginner. Unlocks greetings, politeness, digital actions, and core question words required on Day 1.

👋 Hello ≠ 🚪 Goodbye
Chain 2 · Politeness A0 Level
🙏 Please ≠ 💐 Thank you
Chain 3 · Confirmation A0 Level
✅ Yes ≠ ❌ No
Chain 4 · Inquiry A0 Level
❓ Ask ≠ 💡 Answer
Chain 5 · Oral Communication A0 Level
👂 Listen ≠ 🗣️ Speak
Chain 6 · Literacy A0 Level
📖 Read ≠ ✍️ Write
Chain 7 · Phone Call A0 Level
📱 Call ➔ 💬 Talk ➔ 🤙 Hang up
Chain 8 · Digital Message A0 Level
💻 Open ➔ ⌨️ Type ➔ ✉️ Send
Chain 9 · Power Control A0 Level
🔌 Turn on ≠ 💡 Turn off
Chain 10 · Question Primitives A0 Level
❓ Who ➔ What ➔ Where ➔ When ➔ Why 🧐
🎯 Milestone 1 Can-Do Goal

Can greet others, ask basic question-word queries (Who/What/Where), perform polite requests, and handle initial contact.

Stop Saying These 50 Phrases! Natural Conversational Upgrades for Intermediate English

Break away from repetitive B1 conversational habits with 50 natural phrase upgrades, featuring direct opposites for agreeing, expressing opinions, stating preferences, and asking questions.

Read in: 🇬🇷 EL 🇫🇷 FR 🇮🇹 IT 🇷🇺 RU 🇬🇧 EN
J Written by JY DM
📅 2026-09-13 ⏱️ 11 min read B1–B2 EPISODE 2

When you reach the **intermediate plateau (CEFR B1–B2)**, you can comfortably hold conversations, share opinions, and make yourself understood. However, almost every language teacher notices the same pattern: learners find themselves relying on a tiny cluster of "safe" phrases in every single discussion—defaulting over and over to *"I think..."*, *"I agree"*, *"It depends"*, or *"I don't know"*.

Upgrading these daily expressions doesn't require memorizing complex, obscure idioms. By adopting subtle, natural conversational alternatives—and mastering clear **direct contrasts and opposites**—your English immediately sounds more expressive, warm, and authentic.

Here are 50 overused intermediate phrases broken down into practical conversational categories, complete with natural upgrades and direct opposites where applicable.

1. Agreeing & Disagreeing (Direct Opposites)

Expressing agreement or disagreement is the backbone of active conversation. Instead of defaulting to plain *"I agree"* or *"I disagree"*, try these nuanced alternatives:

1. I agree ↔ 2. I disagree

**Overused Agree:** *I agree.* - **Natural Upgrades:** *I completely agree.* • *Absolutely.* • *I couldn't agree more.* • *That's true.* • *You have a point.*
**Overused Disagree:** *I disagree.* - **Natural Upgrades:** *I'm not so sure.* • *I see it differently.* • *I don't quite agree.* • *I have a different view on this.*

3. It depends

**Overused:** *It depends.*
**Natural Upgrades:** *It varies.* • *It really depends on the situation.* • *It isn't that simple.* • *It comes down to...*

2. Giving Opinions & Stating Uncertainty

Moving beyond a mechanical *"I think"* or blunt *"I don't know"* adds instant polish to your discussions:

4. I think...

**Overused:** *I think...*
**Natural Upgrades:** *I believe...* • *I'd say...* • *In my opinion...* • *From my point of view...* • *It seems to me...*

5. I don't know

**Overused:** *I don't know.*
**Natural Upgrades:** *I'm not sure.* • *It's hard to say.* • *I haven't thought about that.* • *That's a good question.*

6. Maybe

**Overused:** *Maybe.*
**Natural Upgrades:** *Perhaps.* • *Possibly.* • *There's a chance.* • *It could be.*

3. Affirmation & Refusal (Yes vs. No)

Polite conversations rely on varied ways to say yes, no, or of course:

7. Of course

**Overused:** *Of course.*
**Natural Upgrades:** *Absolutely.* • *Definitely.* • *Naturally.* • *Without a doubt.*

8. Yes ↔ 9. No

**Overused Yes:** *Yes.* - **Natural Upgrades:** *Certainly.* • *Definitely.* • *Exactly.* • *That's right.*
**Overused No:** *No.* - **Natural Upgrades:** *Not really.* • *I don't think so.* • *I'm afraid not.* • *Not exactly.*

4. Courtesy, Apologies & Appreciation

Everyday social etiquette sounds much warmer when you expand your polite toolkit:

10. Thank you

**Overused:** *Thank you.*
**Natural Upgrades:** *Thanks a lot.* • *I really appreciate it.* • *That's very kind of you.* • *Much appreciated.*

11. You're welcome

**Overused:** *You're welcome.*
**Natural Upgrades:** *No problem.* • *My pleasure.* • *Anytime.* • *Don't mention it.*

12. I'm sorry

**Overused:** *I'm sorry.*
**Natural Upgrades:** *I apologize.* • *My apologies.* • *I didn't mean to.* • *Sorry about that.*

5. Liking, Disliking & Loving (Direct Opposites)

Instead of relying solely on *"like"* or *"don't like"*, describe your interests with precision:

13. I like it ↔ 14. I don't like it

**Overused Like:** *I like it.* - **Natural Upgrades:** *I'm a fan of it.* • *I'm really into it.* • *I enjoy it.* • *It's right up my alley.*
**Overused Dislike:** *I don't like it.* - **Natural Upgrades:** *It's not really my thing.* • *I'm not a fan.* • *It doesn't appeal to me.* • *I'm not keen on it.*

15. I love it

**Overused:** *I love it.*
**Natural Upgrades:** *I'm crazy about it.* • *I'm obsessed with it.* • *I absolutely love it.* • *I can't get enough of it.*

6. Evaluative Judgments (Good vs. Bad / Interesting vs. Boring)

Broad evaluative adjectives carry far more impact when replaced with precise descriptive alternatives:

16. It's good ↔ 17. It's bad

**Overused Good:** *It's good.* - **Natural Upgrades:** *It's great.* • *It's excellent.* • *It's impressive.* • *It's outstanding.*
**Overused Bad:** *It's bad.* - **Natural Upgrades:** *It's disappointing.* • *It's awful.* • *It's poor.* • *It's terrible.*

18. It's interesting ↔ 19. It's boring

**Overused Interesting:** *It's interesting.* - **Natural Upgrades:** *It's fascinating.* • *It's intriguing.* • *It's thought-provoking.* • *It's captivating.*
**Overused Boring:** *It's boring.* - **Natural Upgrades:** *It's dull.* • *It's repetitive.* • *It failed to keep my attention.* • *It's uninspiring.*

20. It's important

**Overused:** *It's important.*
**Natural Upgrades:** *It's essential.* • *It's crucial.* • *It's vital.* • *It matters a lot.*

21. It's difficult ↔ 22. It's easy

**Overused Difficult:** *It's difficult.* - **Natural Upgrades:** *It's challenging.* • *It's demanding.* • *It's complicated.* • *It's tough.*
**Overused Easy:** *It's easy.* - **Natural Upgrades:** *It's straightforward.* • *It's simple.* • *It's manageable.* • *It's a piece of cake (informal).*

7. Comprehension & Clarification (Direct Opposites)

Clear communication requires smooth signals when listening to others:

23. I understand ↔ 24. I don't understand

**Overused Understand:** *I understand.* - **Natural Upgrades:** *I see.* • *That makes sense.* • *I get it.* • *I follow you.*
**Overused Don't Understand:** *I don't understand.* - **Natural Upgrades:** *I'm confused.* • *Could you explain that?* • *I'm not following.* • *Could you clarify?*

8. Discourse Connectors & Structure

Transition words connect ideas logically and keep long turn-taking fluid:

Basic Connector
Natural Conversational Upgrades
Instead of **25. For example...**
Try *For instance...* • *Such as...* • *To give an example...*
Instead of **26. Also...**
Try *In addition...* • *What's more...* • *Besides...* • *Furthermore...*
Instead of **27. But...**
Try *However...* • *On the other hand...* • *Having said that...* • *Even so...*
Instead of **28. Because...**
Try *Since...* • *As...* • *Due to the fact that...* • *That's because...*
Instead of **29. So...**
Try *Therefore...* • *As a result...* • *Consequently...* • *That's why...*
Instead of **30. Finally...**
Try *In the end...* • *Ultimately...* • *Lastly...* • *To sum up...*

9. Desiring, Needing & Capability (Can vs. Can't)

Differentiate between basic desires, obligations, and physical or situational ability:

31. I want...

**Overused:** *I want...*
**Natural Upgrades:** *I'd like...* • *I'm hoping to...* • *I'm looking to...* • *My goal is to...*

32. I need...

**Overused:** *I need...*
**Natural Upgrades:** *I have to...* • *I'm supposed to...* • *It's necessary for me to...* • *I require...*

33. I can... ↔ 34. I can't...

**Overused Can:** *I can...* - **Natural Upgrades:** *I'm able to...* • *I manage to...* • *I have the opportunity to...*
**Overused Can't:** *I can't...* - **Natural Upgrades:** *I'm unable to...* • *I don't have the chance to...* • *It's impossible for me...*

10. Quantity, Frequency & Time Horizons (Future vs. Past)

Expressing amounts and time perspectives with precision:

35. A lot of...

**Overused:** *A lot of...*
**Natural Upgrades:** *Plenty of...* • *Numerous...* • *A great deal of...* • *Tons of (informal).*

36. Very often ↔ 37. Sometimes ↔ 38. Never

**Overused Very Often:** *Very often.* → *Frequently.* • *Regularly.* • *Quite often.* • *Time and again.*
**Overused Sometimes:** *Sometimes.* → *From time to time.* • *Every now and then.* • *Occasionally.*
**Overused Never:** *Never.* → *Not once.* • *Under no circumstances.* • *At no time.*

39. Every day

**Overused:** *Every day.*
**Natural Upgrades:** *On a daily basis.* • *Day after day.* • *Each day.*

40. In the future ↔ 41. In the past

**Overused Future:** *In the future.* - **Natural Upgrades:** *Going forward.* • *Down the road.* • *Eventually.* • *In years to come.*
**Overused Past:** *In the past.* - **Natural Upgrades:** *Previously.* • *Back then.* • *In earlier times.* • *Once upon a time (stories).*

11. Closing, Questions & Interactions

Wrapping up thoughts, prompting others, and reacting in real time:

42. That's all

**Overused:** *That's all.*
**Natural Upgrades:** *That's about it.* • *That's everything.* • *That's pretty much it.*

43. What do you think?

**Overused:** *What do you think?*
**Natural Upgrades:** *What's your opinion?* • *How do you see it?* • *What's your take on this?*

44. Really?

**Overused:** *Really?*
**Natural Upgrades:** *Is that so?* • *Seriously?* • *No way!* • *You're kidding!*

45. I hope so

**Overused:** *I hope so.*
**Natural Upgrades:** *Fingers crossed.* • *Let us hope so.* • *I'm optimistic.*

46. Good luck!

**Overused:** *Good luck!*
**Natural Upgrades:** *Best of luck!* • *Wish you all the best!* • *I'm rooting for you!*

47. Be careful

**Overused:** *Be careful.*
**Natural Upgrades:** *Watch out.* • *Take care.* • *Stay safe.* • *Be cautious.*

48. Excuse me

**Overused:** *Excuse me.*
**Natural Upgrades:** *Pardon me.* • *Sorry to interrupt.* • *May I ask...?* • *Would you mind...?*

49. I don't care

**Overused:** *I don't care.*
**Natural Upgrades:** *It doesn't matter to me.* • *I'm fine either way.* • *I don't mind.*

50. That's amazing!

**Overused:** *That's amazing!*
**Natural Upgrades:** *That's incredible!* • *That's impressive!* • *That's unbelievable!* • *That's fantastic!*

🌐 Multilingual Adaptations for FR, RU, IT & EL Learners

Every language has its own overused intermediate safety phrases and natural B1–B2 conversational upgrades. Here is how to express these key concepts in French, Russian, Italian, and Greek:

🇫🇷 French (Phrases fréquentes & Opposés)

**D'accord ↔ Pas d'accord:** *Je suis d'accord.* → *Tout à fait.* / *Absolument.* / *Je partage votre avis.* ↔ *Je ne suis pas si sûr(e).* / *Je vois les choses différemment.*
**Je pense...:** *Je pense que...* → *À mon avis...* / *Selon moi...* / *Il me semble que...*
**Ça dépend:** *Ça dépend.* → *Ça varie.* / *Cela dépend de la situation.* / *Ce n'est pas si simple.*
**Je ne sais pas:** *Je ne sais pas.* → *C'est difficile à dire.* / *Je ne suis pas sûr(e).*

🇷🇺 Russian (Частые фразы и противоположности)

**Согласен ↔ Не согласен:** *Я согласен.* → *Совершенно верно.* / *Полностью согласен.* / *Вы правы.* ↔ *Я не совсем уверен.* / *Я смотрю на это иначе.*
**Я думаю...:** *Я думаю, что...* → *На мой взгляд...* / *Мне кажется, что...* / *По-моему...*
**Зависит:** *Это зависит.* → *По-разному.* / *Всё зависит от обстоятельств.*
**Не знаю:** *Я не знаю.* → *Сложно сказать.* / *Хороший вопрос.*

🇮🇹 Italian (Frasi comuni e opposti)

**D'accordo ↔ Non d'accordo:** *Sono d'accordo.* → *Sono assolutamente d'accordo.* / *Hai ragione.* / *Condivido in pieno.* ↔ *Non ne sono così sicuro/a.* / *La vedo diversamente.*
**Penso...:** *Penso che...* → *A mio parere...* / *Secondo me...* / *Mi sembra che...*
**Dipende:** *Dipende.* → *Cambia da caso a caso.* / *Dipende dalle circostanze.*
**Non so:** *Non lo so.* → *È difficile da dire.* / *È una buona domanda.*

🇬🇷 Greek (Συχνές φράσεις & Αντίθετα)

**Συμφωνώ ↔ Διαφωνώ:** *Συμφωνώ.* → *Συμφωνώ απόλυτα.* / *Έχεις δίκιο.* ↔ *Δεν είμαι τόσο σίγουρος/η.* / *Το βλέπω διαφορετικά.*
**Νομίζω...:** *Νομίζω ότι...* → *Κατά τη γνώμη μου...* / *Από τη δική μου πλευρά...* / *Μου φαίνεται ότι...*
**Εξαρτάται:** *Εξαρτάται.* → *Ποικίλλει.* / *Εξαρτάται από την περίπτωση.*
**Δεν ξέρω:** *Δεν ξέρω.* → *Δύσκολο να πω.* / *Καλή ερώτηση.*

Summary Practice: A Natural Upgrade in Action

Notice how replacing just a few generic phrases changes the tone of a real conversation:

“**Before (Repetitive B1):** *"I think this proposal is good. I agree with you, but it depends on the budget. In the future, I want to do this."*”

“**After (Upgraded B2):** *"From my point of view, this proposal is impressive. I couldn't agree more, though it really comes down to the budget. Going forward, I'm hoping to move this forward."*”

Try picking **three phrases** from this list every week and actively applying them during your speaking club or writing practice!
